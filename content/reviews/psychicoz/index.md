## How they keep it honest

This is where PsychicOz separates from the "apply and you're in" crowd.

**A real gate.** Readers submit written applications — experience, specialty, tools, preferred method — then pass identity and account checks, an interview on communication and boundaries, and sometimes a test reading. Not everyone gets through.

**Fourteen rules of ethics.** The code bans guaranteed predictions, fear-based pressure, cross-platform soliciting, asking for extra payments or gifts, and dressing a reading up as medical, legal, or financial advice. In plain terms: the scariest tricks of this industry are banned on paper.

**They watch after hire.** Verified feedback, account activity, complaint patterns, and compliance reviews feed ongoing monitoring. Warnings, limits, suspensions — the platform can act.

**Reviews come from paid sessions.** Eligible, verified readings only. That raises the signal-to-noise on the ratings you're reading.

**A satisfaction guarantee.** If a session goes sideways, you can file through the Resolution Center within a window — the homepage says 48 hours, the about page says 24. Check the current policy before you file. The platform even claims: if a reading brought no value, you won't be charged.

None of this guarantees every sentence lands. Reader fit still varies. Which is why the free three minutes matter.
