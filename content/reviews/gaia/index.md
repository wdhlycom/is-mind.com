Somewhere in the noise, you wanted a quieter room. Not another feed designed to keep you scrolling, but a place to actually sit, stretch, and think about things that don't fit in a news cycle.

Gaia is that room — if you're comfortable with how wide it is.

Here's what it is, what it costs, and where you should keep your eyes open.

## What Gaia is

Gaia calls itself a "conscious media" community. Think of it as a spiritual Netflix: same binge comfort, different shelf. The parent company goes back to 1988, the streaming service launched as Gaiam TV in 2011, and the Gaia brand settled in by 2016. It trades on the Nasdaq under GAIA.

The numbers: 8,000-plus films and series (some counts say 10,000+), over 800,000 paid members across 185 countries, and — this part matters — **no ads, ever, because members pay the bills**. It doesn't sell your data or chase advertisers. That independence shows in what it's willing to publish.

Same line as everything here: entertainment, education, self-exploration. Not a stand-in for medical, psychological, or financial care.

## What you're paying for

Gaia is subscription, not pay-per-reading. New users get a 7-day free trial on either plan, cancel anytime.

| Plan | Price | Note |

| Monthly | ~$13.99–15.99/mo | flexible, no contract |
| Annual | ~$119–139.99/yr | about $9.92–11.67/mo, ~27% off |

Per-member basics: 8,000+ ad-free titles, any device, up to three profiles, unlimited views, a 10% Marketplace discount, and an AI guide trained on Gaia's own library. Cancel and you keep access through the paid period.

## The content — and the spread

This is Gaia's signature and its contradiction. The shelf runs from the provably useful to the proudly fringe:

- **Yoga and meditation** — hundreds of classes, beginner to advanced.
- **Spiritual growth** — manifestation, human potential, mindfulness.
- **Expanded consciousness** — metaphysics, mysticism, channeling, astral projection.
- **Seeking truth** — cover-up series, "Disclosure," secret space programs.
- **Alternative healing** — energy work, longevity, Ayurveda, sound therapy.
- **Ancient origins and the unexplained** — lost civilizations, megaliths, the alien and the supernatural, sacred geometry.
- Plus transformation, health, nutrition, and sustainable living.

You can find a calm 20-minute hip-opener and a documentary on Atlantis in the same afternoon. That range is deliberate.

## The names behind it

Gaia's teacher roster is genuinely first-tier for this world: Gregg Braden, Dr. Joe Dispenza, Caroline Myss, Michael Beckwith, Bruce Lipton, Marianne Williamson, Anita Moorjani, Ram Dass. For the unexplained, Graham Hancock, Freddy Silva, Billy Carson, Jimmy Church. For yoga, Rodney Yee and others. There's even an "Unlimited Tarot Readings" tool now, pulling divination into the library.

Many of these people have decades of books and practice behind them. The production quality sits in the top tier of consciousness media.

## Watching it anywhere

The app covers iPhone, Android, iPad, Apple TV, Roku, and Fire TV, plus the web. iOS and Android let you download for offline — useful on a commute or a flight. Three profiles mean a household doesn't share one recommendation feed. No ads, no view caps. The AI guide will answer questions about what you're watching. And GaiaSphere, the Boulder event center, streams to 185 countries with translation — Gaia+ members get live access and 72-hour replays.

## The honest edges

**The spectrum is a real test of discernment.** Gaia moves from mainstream mindfulness to material that mainstream science calls pseudoscience or worse. Approach the fringe with an open but questioning mind, and keep spiritual exploration separate from medical or scientific decisions.

**Prices float by page.** Monthly and annual show different numbers across sections. Check the current rate and whether the trial is attached before subscribing.

**Gaia+ doesn't refund.** Once charged, unused time usually stays charged. Confirm you actually want the live and workshop perks before upgrading.

**Some deep content assumes its own context.** If you only wanted clean yoga, the volume of fringe material may feel like noise. The category filters are your friend.

## Is Gaia for you?

**You'll probably like it if** you want a real yoga and meditation routine; if you're genuinely curious about consciousness, ancient wisdom, or the unexplained; if you're done with ad-driven mainstream entertainment; if you value offline, multi-device, ad-free viewing; or if you want a global community and live teacher events.

**It's probably not your fit if** you only want pristine yoga and recoil at fringe topics (use the filters, or pick a narrower app); if you need every claim backed by mainstream science (Gaia wears its independent view proudly, and some of it's contested); or if you won't pay upfront for annual or Gaia+.
