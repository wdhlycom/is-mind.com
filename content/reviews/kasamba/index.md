> A stage shows you a performer. A quiet room shows you who's actually listening.

There's a kind of help that doesn't need a camera. You're not looking for a show. You're looking for someone who'll sit with a hard question — your relationship, your dead parent, a pattern you can't shake — and take it seriously.

Kasamba has spent twenty-five years being that room.

Here's what it is, what it costs, what a session actually feels like, and where it stops short.

## TL;DR

What we actually know is this: Kasamba is one of the longest-running online psychic platforms in the world, founded in 1999, and the longest-running text-and-phone platform specifically. ## What Kasamba is

Kasamba launched in 1999. That's over two decades in online psychic consulting — about as senior as this field gets. The platform says it has served millions and gathered "more than 4 million satisfied customers" along the way.

Where Oranum built itself on live video, Kasamba leans the other way: **text chat, phone, and email.** It's built for the deeper, slower spiritual questions — soulmates, past lives, karmic relationships — the ones that need time to unfold. Less a lively broadcast, more a consulting room you can walk into whenever it's open.

Same guardrail: entertainment and self-exploration, adults only, not a replacement for professional care.

## How they vet readers

This is what lets Kasamba call itself "established" without flinching.

**A real screen.** Advisors go through multiple rounds of assessment and ability verification, running since 1999. One third-party review notes a $50 entry fee for applicants — a small economic wall that alone filters out the casual.

**A traceable track record.** Many readers show ten, fifteen, even twenty-plus years on the platform, with thousands of verified reviews. Top advisors display tens of thousands of readings and join dates back to 2004–2014. Long public history is harder to fake than a headshot.

**Transparent pages.** Every advisor profile shows rating, specialty, join year, and feedback — the information you need before you spend.

Quality still varies between readers. Which is exactly why the free minutes below matter.

## How you connect

Three ways:

- **Live chat** — the most popular. Instant, paced, easy to pause and reread.
- **Phone** — warmer, more human, for when you want to be heard.
- **Email** — slow, written, built for the questions worth revisiting. Some complex email readings cost more, by reader.

**One thing to know: no live video.** This isn't a gap in the description — independent reviews agree, and a few aggregator sites wrongly list "video." Trust the official feature set. If face-to-face connection is what you need, [Oranum](/reviews/oranum/) is the better fit.

**The standout perk: three free minutes per new advisor.** Not once for the whole platform — per advisor you haven't tried. You can audition three or four different readers, three minutes each, almost for free, and only then decide who's worth paying. Treat those minutes as an audition, not a reading.

A practical note: the free minutes are credited to your account on first contact. They don't roll over to other readers if you don't use them. So if you try four advisors, you get 4 × 3 = 12 free minutes total. Spend them deliberately.

## What a session actually looks like

A first-time session on Kasamba has a few predictable beats. Knowing the rhythm in advance makes it easier to get value.

**Before you start.** Pick a reader by *the question you actually carry*, not by the prettiest profile. The platform sorts by availability, but you can also sort by specialty, price, and join date. The most experienced readers (10+ years on the platform) are usually a better starting point for a serious question than the cheapest new advisor with five reviews. The price difference is real but not huge, and the experience is.

**The first three minutes.** These are free and they're also the most important. Most readers use them to get oriented: they ask for your name (or a pseudonym), your sign if relevant, and the one question you most want addressed. They don't usually go deep yet. This is the audition, and it's working both ways. You're testing them; they're calibrating to you. Listen for whether they ask good clarifying questions, not just whether they sound warm.

**The paid portion.** Once the free minutes end, the meter starts. A good Kasamba reader will signal where the session is going ("I'm going to look at three threads in this relationship") and will offer a closing summary near the end. They will not pressure you to extend. If a reader does pressure you to keep going or buy credits in the middle of a session, that's a flag — politely end the session, write a fair review, and try someone else with the free minutes.

**What "good" sounds like.** A good reading gives you a specific question you didn't bring in, not just a paraphrase of what you said. If you came in about your relationship, the most useful thing they can do is hand you back a sharper version of the question you were avoiding. That's the work. Generic affirmation is the sign of an average reader, and you can find average on every platform.

**After the session.** You'll get a written transcript automatically (chat sessions), the option to favorite or block the reader, and a prompt to rate and review. The review matters more than it feels like it does — it's how the platform surfaces the long-term good readers, and it's how future clients can find them. Two honest sentences are worth more than five stars with no words.

## What it costs

Pay by the minute, priced by each advisor — so the range is wide:

| Method | Typical range |
|--------|---------------|
| Chat | ~$1.99–4.99/min (top readers to $34.99) |
| Phone | ~$1.99–39.99/min |
| New/entry advisors | often $0.99–1.99/min to build rep |
| Top/specialty | $20–30+/min |

New-user offers stack on top: three free minutes with each new advisor, a first-order discount (commonly 50% off the first paid reading; some promos say 15%), and occasional "deposit $10 to access free minutes with up to three advisors." All low-threshold ways to test the water.

**A satisfaction backstop.** If a session disappoints, Kasamba offers up to $50 in credit to retry another advisor. That's more reassuring than platforms with no refund at all — but note it usually covers only your **first** reading. Choose advisor number one with care.

A practical budget tip: most users find their best long-term reader in the $4–$8/min range after their first few auditions. The $20+/min specialists are worth it for one important question, not for general use. The platform is designed to let you climb the price ladder over time, not to live at the top.

## The honest edges

- **No video.** If you need to see a face and read the energy in a room, this isn't the tool.
- **Top advisors are pricey.** $20–30+ per minute is real money. Use the free minutes to screen before you pick a tier.
- **Five-star ratings are dense — read the text.** With so many high scores, the star alone won't separate "excellent" from "fine." Read the written reviews.
- **The guarantee is first-order only.** Pick your first advisor thoughtfully; the safety net shrinks after.
- **No appointment scheduling.** Most sessions are "online now, chat now." If you need a fixed time, plan around availability.
- **Billing by the minute is unforgiving of slow typing.** Chat reads are billed for the duration of the session, including pauses. If you need to step away, end the session and resume later; don't leave the meter running.
- **Customer support is email, not chat.** Real issues (billing disputes, blocked accounts) take 24–48 hours to resolve. Plan accordingly for any time-sensitive problem.

## FAQ

**Is Kasamba legitimate, or is it one of those scam platforms?**
Kasamba is the oldest major online psychic platform still operating, founded in 1999, with a track record of millions of users and decades of public reviews. The platform itself is legitimate. Individual readers vary, of course, which is why the screening tools and the free minutes exist. The platform is not a substitute for medical, legal, or financial advice — it's entertainment and self-reflection.

**Are the psychic abilities real?**
The honest answer: that's not something a platform review can settle. What we can say is that thousands of people use the platform regularly, many return over years to specific readers, and the long-tenured readers have built reputations that don't appear to be faked. Whether the underlying mechanism is intuition, pattern recognition, or the placebo of being listened to, the experience of feeling heard is real.

**How is the mobile app?**
The iOS and Android apps are functional, not flashy. They handle chat, calls, and account management smoothly. The browser version is more comfortable for long sessions, especially email readings. If you mostly use the platform on a phone, the app is fine; for serious work, a laptop is better.

**What about privacy — do they record sessions?**
Chat sessions are saved as transcripts on your account, accessible to you afterward. Phone calls are not recorded by default, though some readers take written notes during the call. Kasamba's privacy policy follows standard US data-handling practices. They don't sell your data. The main privacy risk is what you choose to share in a session — use a pseudonym for sensitive questions, especially for first contact.

**Can I get a refund if the reading was bad?**
Up to $50 in credit is available for your first session if you're unsatisfied. After that, the platform expects you to use the free audition minutes and review system to find the right reader. There's no general refund policy beyond the first session.

**What if I want to keep working with a reader over months?**
This is where Kasamba quietly shines. Long-term readers on the platform often develop a real understanding of their regulars' lives — they remember the previous conversation, they can track a question over time, and they become a kind of spiritual advisor in the older sense of the word. If you find the right one, the relationship is the value, not any single reading.
