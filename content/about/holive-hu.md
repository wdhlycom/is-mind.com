# Holive Hu — Founder & Editor

Holive Hu founded is-mind.com to explore where modern psychology and ancient symbolic systems — tarot, astrology, energy work, dream interpretation — meet.

His approach is deliberately rational: he uses established psychological frameworks (Jungian analysis, attachment theory, cognitive science) to unpack what these traditions have been pointing at for centuries, in language ordinary people can follow.

## How I work

Every article on this site is **researched, written with AI assistance, and then reviewed and edited by me** before it is published. I treat the AI as a drafting tool — the editorial judgment, the framing, and the final responsibility for accuracy are human, and they are mine.

I do not hold myself out as a licensed therapist, psychologist, or medical professional, and nothing on this site is medical, legal, or financial advice. Content here is for self-reflection and education.

## Corrections

If you find an error, I want to fix it. Reach me through the site and I'll review and correct it — and note the correction on the page.
