# Editorial Policy

## Our purpose

is-mind.com exists to help people make sense of their inner life — using tarot, astrology, energy work, dream interpretation, and depth psychology as lenses, not as literal prophecy.

## Our standards

1. **Grounded, not gullible.** We approach spiritual topics through the framework of modern psychology. We don't claim the cards predict the future or that the planets cause your behavior. We use these systems as tools for reflection.

2. **Plain language.** No jargon for its own sake. If a concept needs a technical term, we explain it.

3. **Honest about limits.** We are not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice. When a topic touches real distress, we say so and point you toward professional help. See our [Medical & Psychological Disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

## How content is produced

Articles are drafted with AI assistance and then **reviewed, fact-checked, and edited by a human editor (Holive Hu)** before publication. AI is a drafting tool; editorial judgment and accountability are human.

## Affiliate relationships

We participate in affiliate programs. Where a recommendation earns a commission, we disclose it — on the page and in our site-wide [Disclosure](/disclosure/). Affiliate relationships never change what we say; criticism stays, praise stays, and you decide.

## Corrections

If you spot an error, tell us. We correct it promptly and note the correction on the page. Our goal is to be more accurate tomorrow than we are today.
