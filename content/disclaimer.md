# Medical & Psychological Disclaimer

The content on is-mind.com is provided for **educational and self-reflection purposes only**.

## What this site is — and isn't

- It is **not** medical, psychological, or psychiatric advice.
- It is **not** a diagnosis or a treatment plan.
- It is **not** a substitute for professional care from a licensed therapist, counselor, physician, or other qualified professional.
- Tarot, astrology, energy work, and dream interpretation on this site are framed as **tools for self-reflection**, not as literal prediction, diagnosis, or treatment.

## When to seek professional help

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, persistent distress, or thoughts of self-harm, please reach out to a qualified professional or a crisis line in your country immediately. A tarot reading or an article is not a replacement for that care.

## No professional relationship

Reading this site does not create a therapist–client, doctor–patient, or any other professional relationship between you and is-mind.com or its authors.

## Use your own judgment

Everything you read here should be weighed against your own circumstances and, where relevant, discussed with a licensed professional. You are responsible for the decisions you make with the information you find here.
